Fix: pricing cards are too small on wide viewports

Current layout caps content at max-width: 1024px. On a 1280px viewport, that leaves ~128px empty on each side. Cards feel cramped relative to the screen.

User feedback: "i see the pricing, but its look small than i expected, there is still many empty space in 2 side"

Trade-offs

  • Proposed A (1400 cap): Big enough at 1280px, still has a cap for very wide screens (>1400px) to prevent the cards from stretching too far. Best balance.
  • Proposed B (no cap): Maximum use of screen space. On ultra-wide screens (>1920px), the cards would stretch to ~600px each which can look weird with the current border-radius.
  • Current (1024 cap): Matches the original mockup. Too cramped on standard laptop screens (1280-1440px).
