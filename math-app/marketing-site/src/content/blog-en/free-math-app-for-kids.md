# Free math app for kids 5-7: how HocVui works

If your kid is 5-7 and starting to learn math, you already know the
options are overwhelming. Most apps want $9.99/month upfront, bury the
best content behind paywalls, or are English-only. Vietnamese families
have it even harder — the few Vietnamese-first apps tend to be either
too academic (drill-and-kill) or too gamified (no actual learning).

**HocVui is free.** No credit card. No trial. No "first 3 lessons then
pay." 50 interactive math lessons per day for every kid, 4 learning
personas, fully bilingual Vietnamese–English. This post explains how
it works and why it's free.

## How HocVui teaches math

Every lesson on HocVui is built around three ideas:

1. **Active, not passive.** Your kid doesn't watch a video or tap
   through flashcards. They manipulate objects on screen — count
   marbles, sort shapes, complete a number line. Research on early
   math (Clements & Sarama, 2021) is clear: building is a stronger
   predictor of later achievement than counting.
2. **Immediate feedback.** Wrong answers get a hint, not a red X.
   The app asks "what if you tried this way?" instead of marking the
   attempt as failed. Especially important for the Struggler persona
   (more on personas below).
3. **Bilingual.** Every word, hint, and instruction appears in both
   Vietnamese and English. The kid picks at app launch, and can
   switch mid-lesson. No second app to install, no second
   subscription.

## The 4 learning personas

HocVui was built around a simple observation: kids learn differently.
A quiz for a 6-year-old Achiever is exactly the wrong format for a
quiet 7-year-old Silent. So before the first lesson, HocVui asks a
short assessment and assigns your kid one of four personas:

- **Achiever** 🏆 — motivated by goals, badges, leaderboards. Best for
  kids who respond to "you earned 3 stars!"
- **Gamer** 🎮 — motivated by play, mini-games, score. Best for kids
  who learn through short, varied challenges.
- **Struggler** 🪜 — needs scaffolding, small chunks, lots of
  encouragement. Best for kids who get frustrated with abstract
  problems. The app automatically decomposes hard questions.
- **Silent** 🤫 — prefers autonomy, minimal narration. Best for kids
  who get overstimulated by sound and celebration. The app
  significantly reduces audio cues for Silent kids.

You can re-take the assessment any time. Most parents find that
their kid's persona matches their school personality.

## What's in the 50 free daily lessons

Each day's 50 lessons are drawn from the full curriculum across
12 grades (currently grades 1-12 are populated; grades 1-3 are
the most thoroughly built out). For a 5-7 year old, the relevant
content lives mostly in:

- **Số học / Numbers** (counting, comparing, basic addition)
- **Hình học / Geometry** (shapes, sorting, spatial reasoning)
- **Đo lường / Measurement** (time, money, length)
- **Tư duy / Logic** (patterns, sequences, simple deduction)

The 50-lesson cap exists to keep screen time reasonable. If your
kid burns through 50 by 3pm, the app shows a soft "Nhắc tôi sau"
banner (Remind me later) and a parent dashboard summary of what
they covered.

## The parent dashboard

Every HocVui account has a parent dashboard, PIN-gated. From the
dashboard, you can see:

- Which lessons your kid completed today, and how long each took
- Their persona and how it affects the lessons they see
- A weekly streak counter
- Their total stars earned (one star per completed lesson, 3 stars
  for perfect accuracy)
- The ability to reset progress, change language, or sign out

The dashboard is intentionally not gamified for parents. It's a
factual summary, not a leaderboard. We want you to see what your
kid is doing, not feel competitive.
