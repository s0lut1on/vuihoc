| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |

## 1. Bé Học Giỏi — The High Achiever

### Profile Recap
- **Name:** Minh Anh
- **Age:** 6
- **Context:** Urban, parents office workers, has own tablet
- **Level:** Above average (counts to 100, simple addition)
- **Motivation:** Praise, badges, being "first"
- **Session Length:** 20-30 mins

### Journey Map

#### Stage 1: Discovery (Parent downloads app)

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Discovery | App Store | Mom searches "học toán lớp 1" | Curious | Too many apps, don't know which is good | Clear screenshots showing grade 1 content, Vietnamese flag badge |
| Discovery | App Store | Reads reviews | Hopeful | Worried app is too easy or too hard | Review highlights mention "adaptive difficulty" |
| Decision | App Store | Taps Download | Excited | App size too big for storage | <50MB download, claim "works offline" |

#### Stage 2: Onboarding (First open)

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Welcome | Splash screen | Sees cute mascot | Happy | Boring loading screen | Animated mascot, quick load (<3s) |
| Setup | Parent gate | Mom enters birth year to confirm | Neutral | Annoying parent gate on every open | One-time setup, remember device |
| Assessment | Skill check | Asked "Can you count to 100?" | Confident | Feels insulted if too easy | Fast-track option: "I'm already good at math!" |
| Assessment | Mini test | Solves 5 problems in 30 seconds | Proud | Gets everything right, bored waiting | Adaptive: if 5/5 correct, immediately unlock harder content |
| Profile | Avatar select | Picks unicorn avatar, enters name | Excited | Limited options | 50+ avatars, rare ones locked until achievements |

#### Stage 3: First Lesson Experience

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Home | Dashboard | Sees "Grade 1 Math" with many locked levels | Determined | Too much locked, wants everything now | Show "Next 3 unlocks" to create goal path |
| Lesson | Counting game | Counts to 50, finishes in 1 min | Bored | Too easy | After 2 easy wins, suggest "Try Hard Mode?" |
| Review | Results | Gets 3 stars + "Perfect!" | Happy but unsatisfied | Wants harder challenge | Immediate prompt: "You are fast! Try Level 2?" |
| Reward | Badge screen | Earns "Speedster" badge | Very happy | None | Animation of badge spinning, confetti |

#### Stage 4: Deep Engagement (Week 1)

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Progress | Leaderboard | Sees rank #3 in class | Competitive | Wants to be #1 | Show "Only 20 points to #1!" |
| Challenge | Hard mode | Unlocks subtraction with borrowing | Excited | Fails first time | Gentle retry: "Even experts need practice!" |
| Discovery | New content | Finds "Math Olympiad" section | Thrilled | Hidden too deep | Feature discovery banner after 5 lessons |
| Parent | Dashboard | Mom sees " Minh Anh is ahead of 90%" | Proud | Wants to share | One-tap share to Zalo/Facebook |

#### Stage 5: Long-term Retention (Month 1+)

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Plateau | Same content | Completes all Grade 1 content | Bored | Nothing new | Auto-unlock Grade 2 preview |
| Social | Comparison | Sees friend got "Golden Star" | Jealous | Wants exclusive items | Seasonal limited badges |
| Renewal | Subscription | Trial ends | Worried mom | Unsure if worth paying | Show usage stats: "Minh Anh learned 50 skills!" |

## 2. Bé Hơi Chậm — The Struggler

### Profile Recap
- **Name:** Hoàng Nam
- **Age:** 6.5
- **Context:** Rural/small city, grandparents care during day
- **Level:** Below average (struggles with 11-20)
- **Motivation:** Needs encouragement, fears failure
- **Session Length:** ~8 mins, gives up easily

### Journey Map

#### Stage 1: Discovery

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Discovery | School teacher | Teacher recommends app to grandma | Neutral | Grandma doesn't know how to download | Simple SMS link, auto-install guide |
| Discovery | Home | Grandma asks neighbor kid to install | Confused | No one to explain | Video tutorial in app with voice instructions |
| Decision | Open app | Sees colorful screen | Curious but anxious | Worried about being tested | Start with play, not test — free exploration mode |

#### Stage 2: Onboarding

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Welcome | Mascot | Green frog waves hello | Slightly happy | New app is scary | Mascot says "Don't worry, we'll learn together!" |
| Assessment | First question | "Count the apples: 1, 2, 3..." | Nervous | Afraid of getting wrong | No timer, no score visible |
| Mistake | Wrong answer | Taps wrong number | Anxious | Red X appears, heart sinks | NO red X — gentle bounce, "Try tapping the apples again!" |
| Success | Correct answer | Counts correctly with help | Happy | Wants to quit while ahead | Immediate celebration, short session praise |

#### Stage 3: First Lesson

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Home | Level select | Sees ALL levels available | Overwhelmed | Doesn't know where to start | Auto-highlight "Start Here" with bouncing arrow |
| Lesson | Number 15 | Confuses 15 and 51 | Frustrated | Thinks he's stupid | Mascot: "15 is 1 ten and 5 ones — here, let me show you!" |
| Break | Timer | App says "Great work! Rest time." | Relieved | Forced to stop | Adjustable timer in parent settings |
| End | Summary | Sees "You tried hard!" instead of score | Proud | Used to being last in class | Growth mindset messaging, no ranking |

#### Stage 4: Building Confidence (Week 1)

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Retry | Same level | Plays same counting game again | Hopeful | Worried it'll be harder | Exact same content until mastery detected |
| Hint | Help button | Taps lightbulb icon | Grateful | Too embarrassed to ask | Unlimited hints, never penalized |
| Progress | Streak | Sees "3 days learning!" | Surpried | Never done anything 3 days straight | Grandma gets notification to praise him |
| Variety | New game | "Let's feed the frog!" (number matching) | Excited | Bored of same game | Unlock new mini-game every 2 mastered skills |

#### Stage 5: Parent Involvement

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Report | SMS/Notification | Grandma gets: "Nam practiced 10 mins today!" | Proud | Can't read long reports | Simple weekly SMS in Vietnamese |
| Guidance | Parent tip | "Nam finds 11-20 hard. Practice counting at home!" | Supported | Doesn't know how to help | Specific, actionable tips, not generic advice |
| Share | Talk at dinner | Says "I fed the frog today!" | Happy | Wants to talk but doesn't know how | Conversation starters in parent notifications |

## 3. Bé Chơi Nhiều Học Ít — The Gamer

### Profile Recap
- **Name:** Bảo Châu
- **Age:** 6
- **Context:** Only child, heavy tablet user, knows YouTube Kids by heart
- **Level:** Average, but distracted
- **Motivation:** Games, rewards, animations
- **Session Length:** Long if gaming, short if learning

### Journey Map

#### Stage 1: Discovery

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Discovery | Friend | Friend shows "cool game" at school | Excited | Doesn't know it's educational | Kid-to-kid marketing, "share your pet" feature |
| Discovery | App Store | Sees game screenshots | Wants to play | Worried it's actually schoolwork | Screenshots show avatars, pets, colorful worlds |
| Install | Open app | Sees math problem first | Disappointed | "Ugh, homework" | Immediate reward: "Tap here to meet your dragon!" |

#### Stage 2: Onboarding

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Avatar | Creation | Designs dragon character | Very excited | Limited customization | Deep customization: colors, accessories, animations |
| World | Home base | Sees "Dragon Island" with empty landscape | Curious | Empty world is boring | 3 starter decorations, promise of more |
| Tutorial | First quest | "Feed your dragon! Solve 3 problems to get food!" | Okay with it | Doesn't want to do math | Short math (3 problems = 1 min), immediate visual reward (dragon eats) |
| Minigame | Unlock | Completes quest, unlocks "Dragon Race" | Super happy | Wants to race without doing math | Force gate: "Practice math to earn race tickets!" |

#### Stage 3: Learning Loop

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Lesson | Math problem | "5 + 3 = ?" | Bored | Wants to race | "Solve 3 problems to earn 1 race ticket!" |
| Speed | Fast finish | Rushes through, taps randomly | Frustrated | Gets wrong, no tickets | Adaptive: if rushing, make questions visual/tap-based |
| Reward | Tickets earned | Sees "+1 ticket" animation | Motivated | Wants more tickets | Variable rewards: sometimes +2 tickets for "extra effort" |
| Race | Minigame | Plays dragon race, beats ghost time | Thrilled | Beats friend record | Share score to class leaderboard |

#### Stage 4: The Grind (Week 1)

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Daily | Habit | Opens app, does 3 problems, plays race | Automated | Wants to skip math entirely | Minimum learning gate: must complete skill to play |
| Unlock | New content | Math unlocks "Fire Dragon Armor" | Obsessed | Wants all items | Transparent progress: "Armor unlocks at 50 problems!" |
| Social | Brag | Shows dragon to friends at school | Proud | Friend has cooler dragon | Referral bonus: invite friend = exclusive item |
| Timer | Parent limit | "15 minutes up! Rest time." | Angry | Wants to keep playing | Visual countdown timer, gentle transition to "see you tomorrow!" |

#### Stage 5: Parent Dashboard

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Report | Stats | Mom sees: "2 hrs game, 15 mins learning" | Concerned | Ratio is bad | Highlight positive: "15 mins = 5 skills learned!" |
| Control | Settings | Mom increases required math before games | Worried | Will child quit? | Gradual adjustment, not sudden lock |
| Buy | In-app | Child begs for "Dragon Pass" | Demand | Wants to spend money | Parent approval for all purchases |

## 4. Bé Nhút Nhát — The Silent Learner

### Profile Recap
- **Name:** Thùy Linh
- **Age:** 6
- **Context:** Shy, new to school, speaks softly
- **Level:** Hard to assess; doesn't participate in class
- **Motivation:** Wants to please parents, fears failure
- **Session Length:** Slow, deliberate, wants privacy

### Journey Map

#### Stage 1: Discovery

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Discovery | Parent | Mom notices child struggles at school | Worried | Doesn't know how to help at home | App marketed as "safe space to practice" |
| Discovery | App Store | Mom searches "math practice for shy kids" | Hopeful | Overwhelmed by options | "No pressure, learn at your own pace" tagline |
| Install | Open app | Linh sits with mom for first time | Anxious | Mom hovering makes it worse | Parent gate: "Let me try alone?" option |

#### Stage 2: Onboarding

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Welcome | Mascot | Soft-spoken owl waves | Calm | Loud/energetic would scare her | Mascot has gentle voice, slow movements |
| Pace | Settings | App asks "Fast or Slow?" | Relieved | Most apps are too fast | Explicit speed preference, defaults to "take your time" |
| Assessment | Test | Given 5 problems, no timer | Calm | Timer would panic | Hidden timer, unlimited time |
| Privacy | Mode | Mom asked: "Share progress with class?" | Declines | Default is "yes" in most apps | Default to PRIVATE mode, opt-in for social |

#### Stage 3: Solo Learning

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Home | Quiet mode | Interface has no numbers/rankings | Safe | Sees others ahead would discourage | No leaderboards, no comparison |
| Lesson | Voice guide | Owl reads every instruction out loud | Comforted | Can't read fast yet | All text voiced, toggle off if preferred |
| Mistake | Wrong answer | Owl: "That's okay, learning takes time" | Relieved | Used to teachers moving on without her | Extra practice on same concept before advancing |
| Success | Right answer | Soft chime, owl nods | Quietly happy | Doesn't like loud celebrations | Subtle positive feedback |

#### Stage 4: Building Confidence (Week 2)

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Progress | Private journal | Sees "You learned 10 things this week!" | Proud | Used to no one noticing | Private achievement log, not public |
| Share | With mom | Decides to show mom her journal | Happy | Wants praise but not attention | Share button generates simple image for mom |
| Growth | Classroom | Raises hand once at school | Thrilled | Still scared, but a little braver | App sends "Courage badge — even trying is brave!" |

#### Stage 5: Parent Dashboard

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Insight | Private note | "Linh is improving but doesn't like time pressure" | Understanding | Doesn't realize child needs slow pace | App detects response time patterns, suggests settings |
| Communication | Chat | App suggests: "Talk to teacher about classroom confidence?" | Empowered | Doesn't know how to advocate | Specific, actionable parent coaching |

## 5. Bé Song Ngữ — The Bilingual Learner

### Profile Recap
- **Name:** Alex / Minh Khang
- **Age:** 6
- **Context:** Mixed family or international school
- **Level:** Average; stronger in one language
- **Motivation:** Parents want math + language practice
- **Session Length:** Normal, language-switcher

### Journey Map

#### Stage 1: Discovery

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Discovery | Parent search | Mom searches "math app bilingual Vietnamese English" | Frustrated | Most apps are English-only or Vietnamese-only | Clearly mark "Song ngữ / Bilingual" on store page |
| Discovery | App store | Finds app, checks screenshots | Curious | Worried UI is mixed language mess | Screenshots show clean language toggle |

#### Stage 2: Onboarding

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Language | Select | Chooses "Math: Vietnamese, Voice: English" | Pleased | Most apps force same language for everything | Granular control: content language vs UI language vs audio language |
| Test | Placement | Answers in English but content in Vietnamese | Confused | Brain switches language mid-problem | Keep language consistent per screen |
| Curriculum | Sync | "Do you follow Vietnamese or US curriculum?" | Confused | Doesn't know the difference | Default to local country, explain differences |

#### Stage 3: Learning in Dual Context

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Lesson | Vietnamese | Reads "Cộng hai số" | Focused | Wants to know English word too | Optional tap-to-translate math vocabulary |
| Voice | English | Hears "Add two numbers together" | Relieved | English audio helps connect concepts | Math vocabulary glossary with both languages |
| Game | Number | Writes "twenty-five" not "25" | Wrong | Format confusion | Accept both, teach convention gently |
| Switch | Toggle | Taps flag icon mid-lesson | Curious | Worried progress resets | Seamless switch, nothing lost |

#### Stage 4: Parent Goals

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Report | Two reports | Sees progress in both languages | Satisfied | Wants to know which language is stronger | Separate progress trackers per language |
| Goal | Setting | Mom sets "50% Vietnamese, 50% English" | Strategic | Hard to track | Auto-balance lessons to hit ratio |
| Testing | Exam prep | Needs practice for Vietnamese school exam | Focused | Content may differ | School-specific practice mode |

## 6. Bé Ở Vùng Sâu — The Offline/Shared-Device User

### Profile Recap
- **Name:** Văn Tùng
- **Age:** 6
- **Context:** Rural, basic phones at home, shared tablet
- **Level:** Varies, school has limited resources
- **Motivation:** Rare screen time = high engagement
- **Session Length:** 30-60 mins bursts, infrequent

### Journey Map

#### Stage 1: Discovery

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Discovery | School | Teacher mentions "free math app" | Curious | No internet at home | Key message: "Works 100% without internet" |
| Discovery | Town center | Dad downloads at internet cafe | Hopeful | Doesn't know how to update later | Download once, works forever, self-contained |
| Install | Shared tablet | 3 kids use same device | Neutral | Other siblings might delete progress | Multiple local profiles with password (color code) |

#### Stage 2: Onboarding (Offline)

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Profile | Selection | Picks red color, enters name "Tung" | Happy | Sister's profile is also there | Simple avatar/color selection, no email |
| Assessment | Offline test | 5 questions to determine level | Calm | Worried about being wrong | No external validation, just for app |
| Download | Content | Chooses which subjects to download | Curious | Storage is limited (8GB tablet) | Modular downloads: "Download counting pack?" |

#### Stage 3: The Rare Session

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Open | App | Hasn't played in 3 days | Excited | Forgets where they left off | Welcome back: "You were learning addition! Continue?" |
| Deep focus | Lesson | Plays for 45 mins straight | In flow | Battery dies, no charger nearby | Optimize battery: reduce animations, dim screen if idle |
| Pause | Interruption | Sister wants tablet | Frustrated | Progress lost | Auto-save every action, resume any time |
| End | Power off | Dad says time to return tablet | Sad but okay | Wants to continue | Clean exit: "Your progress is saved! See you next time!" |

#### Stage 4: Parent Connection (Limited)

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| Report | When connected | App syncs progress when dad goes to town | Delayed | Mom never sees progress | SMS-receipt style report: "Tung mastered counting!" |
| Share | Offline | Daughter tells mom "I got 3 stars!" | Proud | No visual proof | Progress screenshot saved to gallery |
| Support | Help needed | Dad doesn't understand app settings | Confused | No one to ask | All settings have voice explanation in Vietnamese |

#### Stage 5: The Big Day

| Stage | Touchpoint | Action | Emotion | Pain Point | App Opportunity |
|-------|-----------|--------|---------|------------|----------------|
| School | Class | Teacher asks "Who practiced at home?" | Confident | Used to no one raising hand | Recognition matters more than grades |
| Class | Progress | Tùng is better at counting than before | Proud | Friends don't have app | School tie-in: teacher code for progress sharing |
| Home | Request | Asks dad to borrow tablet again | Motivated | Rare opportunity | Reward consistency, not frequency |

## Cross-Persona Journey Matrix

| Stage | High Achiever | Struggler | Gamer | Silent | Bilingual | Offline |
|-------|---------------|-----------|-------|--------|-----------|---------|
| **Onboarding pace** | Fast, skip option | Slow, gentle | Fun first | Very slow, private | Language select priority | Offline-first setup |
| **Mistake handling** | "Try again!" | "It's okay!" | Lose reward chance | No penalty at all | Clarify in both languages | Retry unlimited |
| **Progress shown** | Stars, rank, speed | Effort, streak, growth | XP, items, tickets | Private journal | Dual progress bars | Simple stars |
| **Social features** | Leaderboards ON | Hidden | Friends battle | All OFF | Optional | N/A (offline) |
| **Voice/audio** | Optional | Always ON, encouraging | Energetic | Soft, gentle | Language toggleable | Always ON (can't read) |
| **Session length** | 20-30 min | 8-10 min | 15 min (parent set) | 15 min | 15-20 min | 30-60 min (rare) |
| **Parent report** | Detailed analytics | SMS/voice tips | Time/game ratio | Confidence insights | Bilingual report | Sync when online |
| **Pricing sensitivity** | Medium | High (free critical) | Low (wants items) | Medium | Medium | Very high |
